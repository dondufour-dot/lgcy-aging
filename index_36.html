Suggested weekly cadence
MondayPull fresh queue export. Identify any project that crossed a 30-day threshold since last week — warning to critical, critical to chargeback, chargeback to cancel risk. That becomes the week's priority list.
TuesdayField ops reviews their list — inspection-ready accounts and service-blocked accounts with confirmed scheduled dates. Scheduled dates should be visible in the shared tracker.
WednesdayAccount management reviews their list — lender holds, PFC/SPF status, re-signs in progress. Status update due by end of day.
FridayCompare current week's list to Monday's snapshot. Document what moved, what completed, and what remains stuck. This creates the week-over-week progress record.
